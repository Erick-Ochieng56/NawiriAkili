Services

Professional mental health care

Evidence-based. Compassionate. Confidential. Our services are designed to meet you where you are — and support you toward where you want to be.

This page covers the professional services of Nawiri Akili-Care Africa. Subsidised and free care through the Nawiri Akili Initiative is covered on the Walk With Us page.
Individual & Personal Support
🧠

Individual Therapy & Counselling

Professional, confidential support for individuals navigating emotional, psychological, and life challenges — evidence-based and tailored to you. We work with anxiety, depression, burnout, stress, trauma, grief, life transitions, and more. No presenting concern is too small or too complex.

In-person · Kilifi · Virtual · Trauma-informed · Confidential
👨‍👩‍👧

Family Therapy

Structured support for family systems navigating conflict, communication breakdown, grief, or significant life changes. We work with the family as a whole — helping members understand each other, rebuild trust, and develop healthier patterns together.

Family systems · Intergenerational · Communication · Healing
🫂

Group Therapy & Support Groups

Professionally facilitated therapeutic groups for individuals facing shared experiences — grief, anxiety, trauma, life transitions, or recovery. Group settings offer something individual therapy cannot: the understanding of people who truly know what you are carrying.

Facilitated · Peer support · Shared experience · Structured
🌱

Wellness & Personal Development

For individuals seeking growth beyond crisis. Emotional intelligence, stress management, resilience, self-awareness, and life direction support — for those who want to build, not just recover.

Growth-focused · Resilience · Self-awareness
Organisational & Institutional Services
🏛️

Psychological Consultation

Expert mental health support for organisations, schools, and institutions. We assist with mental health program design, clinical supervision and case consultation, staff wellbeing, and safeguarding policy guidance. We work with institutions to build systems that support people — not just respond to crises.

Schools · Organisations · Institutions · Supervision · Policy · Program design
🏢

Corporate Wellness

Organisations thrive when their people do. We work with workplaces and institutions to build cultures of genuine wellbeing — through employee wellness programs, burnout prevention, mental health awareness training, and CSR-aligned initiatives that create measurable, lasting impact.

Workplace · Workshops · EAP-style · CSR · Burnout prevention

Online · Flexible scheduling · Kenya & international · Secure

Frequently Asked

Questions, answered honestly

How do I know which service is right for me?
Start with an initial consultation. We listen, then guide you toward the most appropriate support — no pressure, no jargon, no judgment.
What if I cannot afford therapy?
Reach out directly. Subsidised and free options exist through the Nawiri Akili Initiative. We will find a way together.
Do you offer virtual sessions?
Yes. All services are available online from anywhere in Kenya or internationally.
Is everything confidential?
Absolutely. All services are held to strict professional and ethical confidentiality standards. Your trust is the foundation of everything we do.
