The Reality We Are Responding To

Mental health care shouldn't cost someone their chance at it.

Over 1 billion people worldwide are living with a mental health condition. Most will never receive care — not because care does not exist, but because it is out of reach. In coastal Kenya, in Kilifi, this is not a distant statistic. It is a person sitting with their pain, alone, because they cannot afford to go anywhere.

The Nawiri Akili Initiative was built to close that gap. Donor funding is the engine. Every contribution is ring-fenced for free care, school programs, and community outreach.
