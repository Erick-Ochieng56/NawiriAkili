Community Impact Arm

Nawiri Akili Initiative

When minds flourish, communities transform.

The Nawiri Akili Initiative is not an extension of the consultancy. It is a dedicated community impact arm — with its own identity, its own mission, and a vision toward registration as an independent Community-Based Organisation. It shares the Nawiri name and the commitment to mental wellness. Everything else is built specifically for community.
Our Purpose

When minds flourish, communities transform

Mental health is not a privilege — it is a right. The Initiative exists to close the gap between that principle and reality: through education, safe conversation, accessible care, and the kind of sustained presence that communities can call their own.

Mental health is not a private burden to be carried alone. It is a shared reality and a shared responsibility — and stigma is what keeps it hidden. The Initiative confronts stigma at every level, replacing silence with education and exclusion with belonging.
From Silence to a Mentally Blooming Community

A pathway walked alongside communities

We do not arrive with solutions. We walk the journey alongside people — from the first conversation to lasting change.

1

Know

Mental health education & awareness. We deliver accessible, culturally grounded mental health education in schools and communities — giving people the language to name what they feel.

2

Speak

Breaking stigma. We create safe spaces — in schools, communities, and online — where mental health is spoken about without shame. No one should have to carry their pain in silence.

3

Reach

Accessible, affordable, and subsidised or free care. Through donor funding and partnerships, the Initiative provides subsidised and free sessions, emergency support, and referral pathways.

4

Grow

School & community programs. Sustained, embedded programs in schools and communities — lasting structures that become part of how a community cares for its own mental wellness.

5

Bloom

Mentally healthy communities. The goal is mentally flourishing communities — where mental health is understood, openly discussed, and actively supported.

What We Are Building

A centre for all minds

The Nawiri Akili Initiative is growing toward registration as a Community-Based Organisation and, in time, a physical and virtual centre — where anyone, from anywhere, can access mental health support at any time, regardless of ability to pay.

Every session funded, every school reached, every conversation that breaks the silence — that is a step toward that centre.
