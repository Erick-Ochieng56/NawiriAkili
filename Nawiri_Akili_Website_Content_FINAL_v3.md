### Who We Are

## A consultancy and a community — built together

Nawiri Akili-Care Africa is a professional mental health consultancy delivering evidence-based therapy, psychological consultation, and organisational wellness rooted in local understanding and global best practice.

The Nawiri Akili Initiative is our community impact arm — extending the mission into schools and communities through education, awareness, and subsidised or free care for those who cannot afford professional support.

Together they form one ecosystem: professional excellence and community access, connected by a shared belief that mental wellness belongs to everyone.

---

### Vision, Mission & Approach

#### Our Vision

A future where mental wellness is understood, accessible, and actively supported across Africa and beyond — where blooming is not the exception but the expectation.

#### Our Mission

To provide professional, evidence-based mental health care, and to extend that care into communities through education, subsidised or free support, and lasting partnerships — for a mentally flourishing community.

#### Our Approach

Evidence-based. Community-centered. Culturally grounded. We build care that belongs here — rooted in African reality, informed by global best practice, and committed to access above all else.

---

### Values

## What guides us

- **Dignity** — Every person who reaches out is met with full humanity — no judgment, no conditions.
- **Access** — Care that cannot be reached is not care. We remove every barrier we can.
- **Community** — Healing happens in relationship. We believe in the collective capacity to hold and uplift.
- **Evidence** — Our practice is grounded in psychological science, adapted to real local contexts.
- **Rootedness** — African in understanding, language, and approach. We build care that belongs here.
- **Openness** — We actively break the silence around mental health — through education and trust.

### Our Model

## How We Work: Profit for Purpose

Nawiri operates as a hybrid social enterprise. Nawiri Akili-Care Africa, our registered consultancy, generates professional revenue through therapy and organisational wellness services. That revenue directly funds the Nawiri Akili Initiative — our community impact arm.

Community sponsorships and partnerships are received and managed through the consultancy while the Initiative's formal CBO registration is being finalised. Every contribution is tracked independently to ensure community-first funds go directly toward subsidised care, school programs, and free community sessions.

We believe professional expertise should fuel social change. Thank you for partnering with us to help communities bloom.

### From Silence to a Mentally Blooming Community

## A pathway walked alongside communities

We do not arrive with solutions. We walk the journey alongside people — from the first conversation to lasting change.

1. **Know: Mental health education & awareness**
   People cannot seek help for what they do not understand. We deliver accessible, culturally grounded mental health education in schools and communities — giving people the language to name what they feel and the understanding that they are not alone.

2. **Speak: Breaking stigma**
   We create safe spaces — in schools, communities, and online — where mental health is spoken about without shame. Because normalising the conversation is the first act of care. No one should have to carry their pain in silence.

3. **Reach: Accessible, affordable, and subsidised or free care**
   Awareness must lead somewhere. Through community sponsorships and partnerships, the Initiative provides subsidised and free sessions, emergency support, and referral pathways. No one is turned away because of what they cannot afford.

4. **Grow: School & community programs**
   Sustained, embedded programs in schools and communities — not once-off events but lasting structures that become part of how a community understands and cares for its own mental wellness.

5. **Bloom: Mentally healthy communities**
   The goal is not just individual recovery. It is mentally flourishing communities — where mental health is understood, openly discussed, and actively supported. Where blooming becomes the expectation, not the exception.
